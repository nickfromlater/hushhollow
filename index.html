Support

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Email hello@wobbet.app and say which device you play on. A person reads every message.

How do I play?

Hold the right side of the screen to pull the plunger back, and let go to launch. Tap the left side and the right side to flip. The first ball of your first run shows you both.

What is free?

The first two chapters, Hush Hollow and Emberwick: fourteen tables and two guardians. One purchase, The Whole Journey, opens chapters three to eight and every winter after. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

I bought the whole journey. How do I get it back?

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings from the title screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. The unlock card has a Restore button too.

The purchase says it is waiting for approval

That is Ask to Buy. The trail opens by itself as soon as the request is approved.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress and stars. Your purchase is never lost: it comes back with Restore purchases.

Does it need the internet?

No. Hush Hollow plays offline. Only buying or restoring the whole journey talks to the App Store.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases at reportaproblem.apple.com.
