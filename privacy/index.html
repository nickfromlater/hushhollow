Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Hush Hollow does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. Your progress, stars and settings are saved only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is sent to us. The only things that leave your device go to Apple: a purchase, through the App Store, and, if you use Game Center, your scores and achievements.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase, The Whole Journey, is made and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's own privacy policy covers that purchase. The app asks the App Store only whether the purchase has been made, so that it can open the chapters.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports your table scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so that they appear on its leaderboards. That happens inside Apple's service, under Apple's privacy policy and your Game Center settings (which also control your nickname and who can see you). We do not receive or keep that information. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Children

Hush Hollow collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub processes basic request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and keep them secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
